words/roots.json (Kitten Caboodle's roots, words, clues and word origins) is made by tools/make-roots.py from two
sources:

Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org/), through kaikki.org's machine-readable copy (https://kaikki.org/): the
meanings of the roots, the clues (each word's definition), where each word and root comes from, and which words are
built on which root. Wiktionary's text is by its contributors and is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The text in
roots.json is shortened from Wiktionary's (first meanings and first sentences of etymologies), and some roots'
meanings are written in plain words where Wiktionary's are about grammar; roots.json is shared under the same
license. Each word in the game links to its Wiktionary page.

SCOWL (http://wordlist.aspell.net/), which decides which words are everyday ones; its notice is in
words/WORDS-LICENSE.txt and must stay with the list.
