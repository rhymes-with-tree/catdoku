# Puzzle ideas

Every puzzle considered for the site, one row each with variants grouped: ideas from earlier chats and the owner's idea list, games the owner plays, the GoComics puzzles, Simon Tatham's collection and the puzzles on wordleunlimited.io. Games already on the site are rows too, so overlaps stay visible.

**Nothing here is a request.** No requests are recorded anywhere in this file. An idea is only wanted once the owner asks for that game, and being mentioned in a chat, an idea list or several places doesn't change that.

**Claude's guesses:** the ease, close to and notes columns are Claude's guesses and assumptions, not the owner's views, except where a note says the owner said or decided something. The old Status column (Yes, Look closer, Option, Idea, No) was also Claude's assumption and has been removed.

Rows are sorted by group, with the games already on the site at the end of each group. When a game ships, set its row's "On the site?" to Yes (or add a row) in the same PR.

- **Clues?** None; Definitions (dictionary definitions would do); Written (needs clues, categories or stories we'd have to write; the owner's rule is that this rules it out unless the owner says otherwise).
- **Claude's ease guess** of adding it: Easy = a simple generator, or mostly reuses a game already on the site; Medium = a new generator plus a solver to guarantee one answer; Hard = needs new art, physics, split-second timing or a big data file.
- **Mentioned in:** where the idea came up (a past chat, the owner's idea list, a games site), so that conversation can be looked back at for anything said about it. It's a pointer to information, not a record of a request.
