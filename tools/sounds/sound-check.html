How this page works

Tap a sound to hear it; the one you tap last in each row is your pick. A dot marks a row you haven't heard yet, and Next in the bar goes to the next one. Hear it in play plays a few seconds of each game with your picks, which shows whether a sound gets tiresome by the twentieth time. Picks stay on this device, and Copy puts them in a message for Claude.

Every sound is made in the browser as it plays, except the ones marked with a pink letter: those are Moonpie, from the owner's recordings. On an iPhone, turn the silent switch off: these play the way the games will, so silent mode mutes them.

Length shortens every sound: each note, thump or swoosh is cut to that share of how long it was, while its notes still start when they did (clicks and ticks stay as they are). The games use 55% now; 100% is how long they were when you picked them.

Send your picks

A splash, a cheer and a crowd are the hardest to make in code. If none of these do, they can come from a free sound library (Kenney, Freesound CC0 or Pixabay), downloaded in a browser.
