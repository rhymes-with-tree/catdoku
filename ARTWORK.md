## Who's who in `cats/`

Most of the cats are just cats, and any game can use them. The `c14-…`, `c15-…` and
`c17-…` to `c20-…` pictures (and any later numbered ones) are these general cats. A few cats
have names, and most have their own files (`name-1.webp`, `name-2.webp`, …). Every named cat can also be used as an ordinary cat
anywhere, but keep track of who they are, because they'll be featured in games of their own.

Purrfect Square puts one of 24 general cats, picked at random each visit, beside its title: those sitting up and facing out
(`c14-63`, `c18-6`, `-7`, `-17`, `-19`, `-21`, `-26`, `-27`, `-32`, `-40`, `c19-1`, `-5`, `-6`, `-14`, `-16`, `-17`, `-28`, `-32`, `-37`, `-40`,
`c20-1`, `-17`, `-18`, `-30`; the list is `SITTERS` in `purrfect/index.html`).

Cat Burglar paints general cats into paintings, picked at random from the 78 that still look like cats when small and
blended in: sitting, standing or crouching, with the face and ears showing (the list is `CATS` in
`catburglar/index.html`). Curled-up balls, backs and cats stretched flat are left out. `c19-21`, peeking round a corner,
is beside its title.

Toy Stash sits one of six general cats on its boxes for each stash (`c19-44`, `c19-45`, `c18-25`, `c19-11`, `c19-2`, `c18-33`, the list `GUARDS` in `stash/index.html`); once the stash is found, `c17-17` (batting a ball) or `c19-43` (pouncing on a feather) plays with the toys, and `c17-18` (pouncing on a ball) is on the gold-paw card.

Cat Pile lays the napping cats from Sunbeams (the `SLEEPERS` list, the same in both pages) on its clumps' blankets, a straight one or a round one in each spot, and `c14-36` curls up beside its title.

- **Jan Brady** (`jan-brady-…`, fluffy black and white, long dark tail; he's a he) is a one-of-a-kind
  character. The `c16-…` pictures, the fluffy black and white cats napping in Sunbeams, are
  Jan Brady too (they keep their numbered names because the games already use them). He's the
  mascot of Tail Chase (`jan-brady-face`, cut from `jan-brady-27`, is the runner at the head of the yarn, `jan-brady-14` crouched beside the title,
  `jan-brady-34` leaping on the gold-paw card) and of Hide & Seek (he hides among the cats in the
  boxes as `c16-3`, `c16-5`, `jan-brady-15` and `jan-brady-33`, his tail is up beside the title in
  `jan-brady-22`, and `jan-brady-31` looks up from the gold-paw card).
- **Higgins** (`higgins-…`, brown tabby) is a one-of-a-kind character too, and the mascot of
  Catdoku: `higgins-1` sits beside the title, `higgins-face` (cut from it) is every cat on the board, in the counter, the walkthrough and the cat facts, and `higgins-2` stretches out on the gold-paw card.
  The other tabbies on the Higgins sheet (`c20-…`) are general cats. Higgins hosts Treat Time too: `higgins-1` sits beside its title,
  the hungry cats at the ends of the rows and columns are the sitting tabbies from the sheet, and `c20-3` sleeps it off on the gold-paw card.
- **Fred** (`fred-…`, white with tabby patches, blue collar with a star tag) is the mascot
  of Knock It Off: `fred-1` sits beside the title, `fred-14` bats a toy mouse on the gold-paw
  card, and the cat on the table is Fred drawn from above (slender and white, with a tabby cap split by a white
  blaze, white ears, one tabby patch behind the left shoulder and a sky-blue collar, as in photos of him). `kitten-star.webp`, the kitten freed in Bubbles, is Fred too
  (sometimes called Fred Kitten). Fred is the star of Pick of the Pile too: `fred-7` sits beside its title, `fred-11` curls up asleep on
  the rug once the pile of toy blocks is gone, and `fred-22` leaps on the gold-paw card.

  mascots of Twirl and Tunnel Run, and will nap in Sunbeams. In Twirl, `bugsy-6` curls up beside the

  tunnel in the middle of the rug and does the winning run (`bowtie-6`), and Bugsy leans in over the rug

  tunnel (for Tunnel Run); `bowtie-7` is the same tunnel with a second Bowtie rolling over beside
