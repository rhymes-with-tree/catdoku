## Who's who in `cats/`

**Who gets along:** Fred and Jan Brady are friends, and Gus, Peanut, Marmalade and Willow are all friends with each other. Fred, Dottie, Jan Brady and
Higgins can appear together, but of those four only Fred and Jan get along: put Dottie or Higgins in a picture
with the others and they shouldn't look friendly (no cuddling or playing together).

Most of the cats are just cats, and any game can use them. The `c14-…`, `c15-…` and
`c17-…` to `c20-…` pictures (and any later numbered ones) are these general cats. A few cats
have names, and most have their own files (`name-1.webp`, `name-2.webp`, …). Every named cat can also be used as an ordinary cat
anywhere, but keep track of who they are, because they'll be featured in games of their own.

Purrfect Square puts one of 24 general cats, picked at random each visit, beside its title: those sitting up and facing out
(`c14-63`, `c18-6`, `-7`, `-17`, `-19`, `-21`, `-26`, `-27`, `-32`, `-40`, `c19-1`, `-5`, `-6`, `-14`, `-16`, `-17`, `-28`, `-32`, `-37`, `-40`,
`c20-1`, `-17`, `-18`, `-30`; the list is `SITTERS` in `purrfect/index.html`).

Cat Burglar paints general cats into paintings, picked at random from the 78 that still look like cats when small and
blended in: sitting, standing or crouching, with the face and ears showing (the list is `CATS` in
`catburglar/index.html`). Curled-up balls, backs and cats stretched flat are left out. `c19-21`, peeking round a corner,
is beside its title.

Toy Stash sits one of six general cats on its boxes for each stash (`c19-44`, `c19-45`, `c18-25`, `c19-11`, `c19-2`, `c18-33`, the list `GUARDS` in `stash/index.html`); once the stash is found, `c17-17` (batting a ball) or `c19-43` (pouncing on a feather) plays with the toys, and `c17-18` (pouncing on a ball) is on the gold-paw card.

Cat Pile lays the napping cats from Sunbeams (the `SLEEPERS` list, the same in both pages) on its clumps' blankets, a straight one or a round one in each spot, and `c14-36` curls up beside its title.
