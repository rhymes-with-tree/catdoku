Toy Box Tango

Share two toys out fairly

Fill every square with one of the two toys: tap once for the first toy, twice for the second, and again to empty it.

Every row and every column has the same number of each toy, and no three of the same toy sit next to each other in a line. An = between two squares means they hold the same toy; an × means they're different. Squares that break a rule are outlined in red. Every puzzle has exactly one answer.
