Knock It Off

Push everything off the table

Walk into things to push them. Push them past the edge and they fall off. You can only push, never pull, and only one thing at a time.

Lemons and piles of books won't budge (cats can't stand citrus). Yarn and pencils roll until they hit something. Grey walls stop things falling. Keep your food bowl on the table.

Keep the candle away from books. If it falls, knock off a glass of water or a vase within 6 moves to put out the fire.

Swipe or use the arrow keys to step, or tap a square to walk there. Clear the table in the fewest moves possible for a gold paw.
