Catwalk

Build a gigantic cat tower: link every perch with catwalks

Each perch needs exactly as many catwalks as its number. Tap a perch, then any other perch, to build a catwalk between them, at any angle. Do the same again for a second catwalk, and a third time to take them away.

A catwalk can't go through another perch. Build wherever you like while you work it out; in the finished tower no catwalks cross, at most two join the same pair of perches, and the cats can reach every perch: it all joins into one tower.
