Catwalk

Build a gigantic cat tower: link every perch with catwalks

Each perch needs exactly as many catwalks as its number. Tap a perch, then another perch straight across or up and down from it, to build a catwalk between them. Do the same again for a second catwalk, and a third time to take them away.

Catwalks never cross each other, and at most two join the same pair of perches. When you're done, the cats must be able to reach every perch: it all has to join into one tower. Every tower has exactly one answer.
