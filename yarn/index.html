Yarn

For color-blind play
StartEnd

Help the kitten lead one strand of yarn through every ball of yarn, lightest to darkest, passing through every square exactly once. The skein at the top shows the order.

Start on the lightest ball and drag. Drag back to take yarn back, or tap any square on the strand to cut it there.

Small boards use a whole colorway, so each ball looks clearly different. Bigger boards use a narrower slice of it, so neighboring shades get harder to tell apart.
