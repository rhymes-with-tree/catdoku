Nip Trip

Zen out and play with colour

  • Two colours two steps apart mix into the one between them.
  • Opposites make mud, which never mixes.
  • Two of the same colour join into one, and two muds vanish.
  • Next-door colours don't mix.
Board settings

Tap a block, then a block next to it (up, down or sideways). If they mix, the new colour lands on the second block, and the blocks above the first one drop down, with a new block falling in at the top.

When a whole row is one colour it clears, and everything above drops. A row of mud clears too. There's no timer and no goal. If no blocks can mix, they swirl into a new order. Your board is kept for next time.
