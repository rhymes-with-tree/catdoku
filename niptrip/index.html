Nip Trip

Zen out and play with colour

  • Two colours two steps apart mix into the one between them.
  • Opposites make mud, which never mixes.
  • Two of the same colour join into one, and two muds vanish.
  • Next-door colours don't mix.

Pick your colours

Tap one in each column, or the rainbow square for any colour you like. The board changes as you go, and your colours are kept on this device.
