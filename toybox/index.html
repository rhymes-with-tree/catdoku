Toy Box

Put every toy back in its basket

Tap a basket to tip its front toys onto the ball track. If the two front toys differ, tap the one you want to send. Each toy drops into the first basket it reaches whose front toy matches, or the first empty basket, so time your taps. Toys go once round the track before they can go back into their own basket. Every level can be won if you time it right.

Fill a basket with eight of one toy and the basket is carried off, toys and all. If the track fills up and no toy has anywhere to go, the track jams.
