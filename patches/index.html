Patches

Sew a four-fabric quilt

Sew a quilt: give every patch one of four fabrics so no two patches that share a seam match. Patches may touch at a corner.

Patches with a button are already sewn and cannot be changed or moved. Pick a fabric, then tap or drag across patches. Turn off Fabric patterns for plain colors. Tap a patch again to take it off. Matching patches side by side get red hatching.
