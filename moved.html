Catdoku is now Lap Cat Puzzles, at lapcatpuzzles.com. Your scores, streaks and games in progress can come too.

Playing from your Home Screen? Your progress is kept inside this icon, so bring it over like this:

  1. Tap Copy my progress code.
  2. Open lapcatpuzzles.com in your browser and add it to your Home Screen.
  3. Open the new icon, go to Settings, and paste the code under Bring over your progress.

Once it’s there, you can delete this old icon.
