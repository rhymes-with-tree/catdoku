                How these are counted
                Visits
                GoatCounter counts each page or moment once a visit, with no cookies and nothing about the player, so every number here is a count of visits. Someone who switched off Lend a paw in Settings, or whose browser blocks the counter, isn't counted.
                Started
                A moment with "new puzzle", "new board", "started" and the like in its name. A saved puzzle picked up again isn't one.
                Solved, lost
                Moments with solved, won, done, finished, cleared and the like, and with fell, out of moves, stuck, overflowed, gave up and the like.
                Dismissing a note
                Dismiss moves a note into the folded group while you're looking. To dismiss one for good, ask Claude, and it's kept as Dismissed.
                Suggested fixes
                Written by Claude when the numbers were last pulled, from the feedback sheet, the errors and the site's code and history. Names and contact details from the sheet are left out.
                Fresh numbers
                Ask Claude in the Catdoku repository to update Pawprints.
